# Ganjuko Mountain Kit 1

High-country pieces: rock formations, snow-dusted variants, a shrine gate,
and a working mine set. The combined sheet (`sheets/GanjukoMountain_B.png`)
contains this kit **plus the shared commons** (rocks, pine, grass tuft).

- **Sheet:** `sheets/GanjukoMountain_B.png` (768x768, B slot)
- **Fragment:** `tileset-fragment-mountain.json`
- **Layout map:** `sheetmap-mountain.json`
- **Catalog:** `../../library.json` (`biome: "mountain"` / `"commons"`)

## Contents

- **rocks** — tall craggy formation, thin rock spire, cliff face section
  and a star-flag cliff overhang (shades the tile below it), cairn.
- **snow variants** — snowy pine (the biome's signature tree), snow-capped
  large rock, snow boulders, icicle rock, a walkable snow drift. Snow
  versions of commons pieces are deliberate: same silhouettes, alpine dress.
- **trees** — snowy pine, bare dead tree, small snowy pine, alpine flowers.
- **landmarks** — vermilion torii-style gate (walk through the middle
  cell), mountain shrine (counter front), stone lantern, prayer flags
  (star overhead), lookout pole and platform, hot spring basin, wooden
  stairs (walkable), rope bridge (walkable) for chasms.
- **mine** — timber-framed mine entrance, support beams, scaffolding,
  mine cart on rails, a blank warning sign, and a glowing crystal cluster
  that also seeds the future Cave Kit.
- **paths** — gravel patch and scree slope (flat, walkable).
